Bravo Groups

Measure a group, and find out what it actually tells you.

Bravo Groups records a session, lets you mark each shot, and works out the geometry: group size, mean radius, dispersion statistics, and the scope correction that would move the group’s centre onto the point of aim. The analysis is pure mathematics in a testable package — no image processing guesswork, no smoothing of results you’d rather not see.

The statistics are deliberately not flattering. A three-shot group is a weak estimate of anything, and the app is happy to say so.

Privacy#

Bravo Groups collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no account.

Sessions, marks and any photos you attach stay on the device. Nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload it to.
