Bravo Timer

A shot timer driven by what it hears.

The detector is real signal processing rather than a volume threshold: a pure-DSP core that consumes audio chunks and emits timestamped shot events, with an AVAudioEngine front end feeding it live from the microphone. The core is offline-testable, and it was tuned against actual range recordings using a command-line harness built for the purpose — which is the only honest way to know whether a detector works.

Privacy#

Bravo Timer collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no account.

The app needs microphone access to detect shots. Audio is processed on the device, in memory, purely to find shot events — it is not recorded, not stored, and not transmitted. Your timing sessions stay on the device.

Support#

If shots are being missed or double-counted, tell me the firearm, the rough distance from the phone, and whether you were under a covered firing line — reverberation is usually the culprit.
