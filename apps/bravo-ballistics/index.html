Bravo Ballistics

A ballistics calculator that does the physics properly and then gets out of the way.

Under the interface is a dependency-free solver: a 3-DOF point-mass trajectory integrated with Runge–Kutta, plus the empirical corrections that separate a toy from a field tool — spin drift, aerodynamic jump, Coriolis, line-of-sight angle, and powder-temperature muzzle velocity shift. Drag is modelled against G1, G2, G5, G6, G7, G8 and RA4.

The solver emits true physical angles and signs. A separate layer translates those into what you actually dial: come-ups, turret units, and your scope’s tracking. That translation is pinned by tests rather than by comments, because sign conventions are where this kind of software quietly goes wrong.

Built to be read outdoors#

Theming is app-owned rather than following the system switch — Dark, Light and Desert — because the right contrast on a range in flat noon light is not the one your phone picks for you.

Privacy#

Bravo Ballistics collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifier, no account.

Your rifle profiles, loads and environmental data are stored on the device. Nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload it to.
