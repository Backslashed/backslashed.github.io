Gungnir

Know how much to dial before you take the shot.

Pick your rifle, enter the distance and the wind, and Gungnir gives you elevation and windage in mils or MOA. Dial it, or hold it. It takes care of the small things too: spin drift, aerodynamic jump, shooting uphill or downhill, and how your powder reacts to temperature.

What’s here#

  • Your rifles: each one keeps its scopes and loads together, so switching load brings the right zero and velocity with it

  • 1,500 bullets built in, with their BCs, and room for your own

  • Today’s weather for where you’re standing, or your own readings

  • Truing: tell it where your shots landed at distance and it works out your real muzzle velocity or BC

  • Range cards to save as a PDF or an image and tape to the stock

  • Matches: set up every stage and target before the match, and the numbers are waiting when the clock starts

Everything works on the sample rifles that come with the app. One purchase lets you add your own. You pay once, and there’s no subscription.

Privacy#

Gungnir collects nothing. It has no analytics, tracking, advertising identifier or account.

Your rifles, loads and matches are stored on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, they sync between your devices through your own private iCloud storage, which I can’t see. There is no server of mine for them to go to.

The app only uses your location when you ask for the weather where you are. It then sends your coordinates to Open-Meteo to get the temperature, pressure and humidity, and to Apple to put a place name on them. Picking a spot on the map works the same way. Your location isn’t stored, and nothing is sent when you type the weather in yourself.
